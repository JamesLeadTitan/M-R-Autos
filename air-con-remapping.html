How It Works

What To Expect

One job you should do every couple of years, and one you should think about first.

Air conditioning loses refrigerant slowly even when nothing is wrong with it, which is why systems that have never been serviced usually blow cool rather than cold. A service evacuates what is left, checks the system holds pressure, recharges it to the correct weight and tests the output at the vents. It also keeps the seals and the compressor lubricated, and a seized compressor is a far larger bill than a regas.

The other reason to use it year round is demisting. Air con dries the air, so it clears a fogged windscreen much faster than heat alone on a wet Herefordshire morning. If yours has stopped working entirely, that is usually a leak or an electrical fault rather than simply low gas, and we will find out which before quoting.

Remapping is a different sort of job and deserves a proper conversation. Adjusting the engine management can genuinely improve drivability, particularly mid-range pull on a turbo diesel, and can help economy if your driving changes to suit. But it is not free of consequences: it can affect a manufacturer warranty and it is a modification your insurer needs telling about. We would rather explain all of that up front than have you find out later.
