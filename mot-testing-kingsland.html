Kingsland Drivers

Local MOT Testing You Can Trust

Kingsland sits about four miles north west of us on the A4110, which makes it one of the quickest runs into the workshop of anywhere we cover. Most people drop the vehicle in the morning on their way through and pick it up on the way back.

Village mileage is harder on a vehicle than the numbers suggest. Lanes around Kingsland are narrow, edged rather than kerbed, and carry a fair amount of farm traffic, so suspension bushes, tyre shoulders and exhaust mountings take more punishment than they would on a town car. That is what we look at first when a Kingsland vehicle comes in with a knock or an odd noise.

We see plenty of higher mileage diesels from this direction, run by people commuting towards Hereford or Ludlow. Those engines are generally happy as long as the servicing keeps up with them, and we would rather tell you a service is due early than deal with the consequences later.
