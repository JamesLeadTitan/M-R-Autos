How It Works

What To Expect

An MOT should take an hour of your day, not a week of chasing.

You drop the vehicle with us at the booked time and we get straight on with the test. A Class 4 MOT covers cars, small vans and camper vans up to 3,000kg, and the inspection runs through brakes, steering, suspension, tyres, lights, exhaust emissions, the structure of the vehicle and everything else on the DVSA list. Nothing is skipped and nothing is padded out.

When the test is done we go through the certificate with you properly. A pass with advisories is the most common result, and advisories matter, because they are the tester telling you what is going to fail next year. We will tell you which ones need booking in soon and which ones can genuinely wait, rather than turning the whole list into a bill.

If something fails, you get a straight explanation of what it is and why it did not pass, followed by a price for putting it right. Because the workshop is on the same site as the test bay, a failure usually means one visit rather than two, and the retest happens here once the work is finished.
